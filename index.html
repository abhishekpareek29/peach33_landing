The AI you have isn't yours.

Today's AI tools are built around tasks, not people. They forget you between sessions, share infrastructure with strangers, and treat your life as a series of prompts.

Peach33 is the opposite: one always-on AI, provisioned just for you, that remembers what matters and works in the background. The infrastructure is finally cheap enough to give every person their own dedicated agent. We think that changes what an AI can be.

  • Morning, decoded.

Questions.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?
Those are tools you visit. Peach33 is an agent provisioned just for you — your own machine, your own memory, always running. It works in the background while you do other things.
Is my data private?
Each user gets their own isolated machine. Your conversations and memory live on infrastructure dedicated to you alone. Nothing is shared. Nothing trains a public model.
What can it actually do today?
Chat, voice, scheduled tasks, email and calendar, web research, drafts and replies. More capability rolls out as the beta progresses.
