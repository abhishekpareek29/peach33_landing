Today's AI tools are built around tasks, not people. They forget you between sessions, share infrastructure with strangers, and treat your life as a series of prompts.

Peach is the opposite: one always-on AI, provisioned just for you, that remembers what matters and works in the background. Infrastructure is finally cheap enough to give every person their own agent.

Questions.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Those are tools you visit. Peach is an agent provisioned just for you: your own machine and your own memory. It works in the background while you do other things.

Is my data private?

Your assistant runs on its own machine, and nothing you share with Peach is shown to other users. Your conversations and memories are stored in our database, hosted by Fly.io in the US, and sent to Microsoft Azure, which runs the AI models, to generate replies. We don’t train AI models on what you share. The details are in our privacy policy.

What can it actually do today?

Chat, a memory you can see and edit, routines and reminders, searching the web and reading web pages, and answering questions about documents you upload. More capability rolls out as the beta progresses.
