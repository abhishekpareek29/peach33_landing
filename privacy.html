Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data the Peach app, the service behind it and the peach33.com website collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

Peach is in an invite-only beta, distributed through Apple TestFlight. This policy describes how Peach works today. Some features described on our home page are not available yet. Before we turn on a feature that changes how we handle your data, we will update this policy.

In short

  • We collect your email address, a password (stored only as a salted hash) and the things you share with Peach, your assistant.
  • To answer you, Peach sends your messages and the content a task needs to Microsoft Azure, which runs the AI models. The app asks for your permission before this happens.
  • Your data is stored in a database hosted by Fly.io in the United States.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not track you. We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Who we are

Peach is operated by [[OWNER LEGAL NAME]], a sole proprietor based in [[CITY, STATE/REGION, COUNTRY]] (“Peach33”, “we”, “us”). We decide how and why your personal data is processed, which makes us the data controller.

This policy covers the Peach iOS app, the online service it connects to, and the website at peach33.com. Questions go to support@peach33.com.

Data the app and service collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password. We store the password only as a salted bcrypt hash, never the password itself.
  • An optional display name.
  • A time zone setting (currently always UTC), used to schedule your routines and reminders.
  • Account status, such as when the account was created.
  • Sign-up is by invitation. For each invitation we keep the invited email address and a hashed copy of the invitation code.

Your assistant’s settings

The name and look you choose for Peach, your progress through setup, which abilities you allow Peach to use (for example memory, routines, reading web pages and reading your documents), and which kinds of actions Peach must ask you about first.

Conversations

The messages you send, Peach’s replies (including the working notes the AI produces while answering), conversation titles, and summaries Peach writes to keep track of long conversations. When you send a message, a working copy is kept so it can be delivered reliably to your assistant, for up to 7 days after your assistant finishes with it.

Memories

If you allow Peach to use memory, it saves short facts about you so it can remember them later, along with a link to the conversation each fact came from. You can view and delete these memories in the app. Deleting a memory does not remove it from the conversation where you first mentioned it; to remove that, email us.

Routines, reminders and notifications

If you allow routines, Peach saves the routines and reminders you ask for (what to do and when), along with each run’s full transcript, which is stored like a conversation, and a summary of the run. Results are delivered as notifications inside the app. If you allow notifications, reminders and routine results also arrive as push notifications. Each push carries the notification’s title (up to 120 characters), the first 240 characters of its text, a notification ID and a link into the app.

Documents you upload

You can upload a text, Markdown or PDF file (up to 2 MB each) so Peach can answer questions about it. We extract the file’s text, split it into passages, and create embeddings: lists of numbers that let Peach find the passages relevant to a question. We store the file name, type, size, a fingerprint (hash) of the file, the extracted text, the passages and their embeddings. We do not keep the original file.

Web pages and web search

If you allow Peach to read the web, it can fetch public web pages and search the web for you. Our servers make these requests, so websites and the search provider see our server’s address rather than yours. To search, Peach sends search terms it writes for your request to [[WEB SEARCH PROVIDER: Brave Search or Microsoft Azure, whichever production uses]]. What Peach reads, including search results, becomes part of your conversation.

Connected accounts

Connected accounts, such as email or calendar, are not currently available. If we add them, connecting one will be your choice, and we will update this policy before the feature is available.

Technical and usage data

  • Sign-in sessions. The app holds a short-lived access token (30 minutes) and a refresh token (30 days). We store refresh tokens only as hashes.
  • Usage records. For each AI request we record which model was used and why, the number of tokens, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and internal reference numbers. These records contain no message content. We use them to run usage limits and to understand costs.
  • Abuse protection. To limit repeated attempts and requests, we keep short-lived counters keyed by a keyed hash of your IP address, email address or account reference number. They are deleted about a day after their time window ends. We also count your daily use of Peach’s abilities to apply usage limits. We do not store the IP address itself in our database.
  • Server logs. Our service logs technical events: times, request and account reference numbers, status codes, timings and token counts. Our main service’s logs are designed to leave out message content and replace IP addresses and requested addresses with placeholders. Error logs from your assistant’s machine may occasionally include fragments of content. Our hosting provider, Fly.io, handles your IP address at its network edge to deliver traffic to us.
  • Operational records. Records of setting up and removing your assistant’s machine, and a log of administrative actions taken on the service.

Your assistant’s machine

Each account gets its own virtual machine, hosted by Fly.io, that runs your assistant. It has no permanent storage of its own: your data is kept in our central database, also hosted by Fly.io, and the machine only holds what it is working on.

What the app does not collect

The only iOS permission the app may ask for is to send you notifications. If you allow notifications, the app sends us the push token Apple assigns to the app on your phone, plus an ID for this installation of the app. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and apart from the push token, it reads no advertising identifier or other device identifier. Apart from the technical information any internet connection carries, it does not send your device model, operating system version or language settings to us. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. If you use TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and feedback with us, which include your device model and iOS version (see Who we share data with).

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them to reply.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data in the iOS Keychain, which iOS protects. These items are available only while your device is unlocked, stay on that device and are not synced to iCloud:

  • your sign-in tokens;
  • messages you have written that have not yet reached our servers, plus delivery receipts (the text is removed once the server accepts a message);
  • reference numbers of requests and approval decisions the app is still tracking;
  • a record that you accepted the AI notice: your account’s reference number and the notice version.
  • [[CONFIRM WITH #153: if the app keeps the installation ID (client_device_id) in the Keychain, describe it here; otherwise delete this item.]]

Your conversations, memories, routines and settings are loaded from our servers when you use the app and are not saved to your device’s storage.

Signing out removes your sign-in tokens; messages you wrote that never reached our servers stay on the device for that account. Deleting your account also tries to remove those unsent messages and tracking data, but leaves the AI-notice record. iOS may keep Keychain items after you delete the app.

AI processing by Microsoft Azure

Peach uses AI models run by Microsoft Azure to understand your requests and write its replies. For each request, we send Microsoft Azure:

  • your message and the recent conversation, including earlier summaries;
  • your saved memories, your assistant’s name, the current time and your time zone, and your routines when relevant;
  • the content a task needs, such as passages from documents you uploaded, text from web pages Peach reads, and web search results;
  • passages from documents you upload, so Azure can create their embeddings.

We do not include your account email address or password in these requests.

Microsoft processes this content as our service provider to generate Peach’s responses. [[CONFIRM AND STATE: how long Microsoft Azure retains prompts and outputs for this deployment (for example for abuse monitoring), and whether Microsoft may use them to train or improve models.]] We do not train AI models on your content.

Before you first chat with Peach, the app tells you about this and asks you to agree to sending this content to Microsoft Azure. Your answer is stored only on your device. Peach cannot work without this processing: if you do not agree, sign out. To stop this processing later, stop using Peach and delete your account.

Peach’s replies are generated automatically. We do not use automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

How we use data

  • To provide Peach: to create and run your account and assistant, answer your messages, remember what you asked it to, run your routines and search your documents.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, including sign-in limits and daily usage limits.
  • To operate, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the service.
  • To respond to you when you contact us, and to tell you about important changes to the beta.
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights and the safety of others.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that run Peach for us, and only what each needs:

  • Microsoft Azure runs the AI models, as described above.
  • Fly.io hosts our service, your assistant’s machine, and our database and its backups in the United States (Ashburn, Virginia), and processes our server logs in the United States.
  • Apple distributes the beta through TestFlight. Apple handles your invitation and installation under Apple’s own privacy policy and makes certain beta information available to us, such as the email address you were invited with (if we invited you by email), install and session counts, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers push notifications if you allow them, so it receives each notification’s content and your push token.
  • [[WEB SEARCH PROVIDER]] runs web searches for Peach, so it receives the search terms Peach sends (see Web pages and web search).
  • [[EMAIL PROVIDER]] hosts our support mailbox and the email account we use to send beta invitations, so it processes the emails you send us and your email address if we invite you by email.
  • GitHub (with its content delivery network, Fastly) and Formspree serve the peach33.com website and its waitlist. See Data the website collects.

Microsoft Azure and Fly.io process data on our behalf under data-processing terms that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy describes. Websites that Peach reads for you, and the search provider, receive requests from our servers, as described above. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or where needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us. Formspree emails each sign-up to us at [[WAITLIST NOTIFICATION INBOX PROVIDER]]. We use your email address only to invite you to Peach or tell you when your spot opens. Fonts and hosting

The site serves its own fonts, so no font service receives your IP address. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, served through Fastly’s network; GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

No cookies or trackers

The website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

How long we keep data

  • Account, settings, conversations, memories, routines, notifications and documents: until you delete them or delete your account. Nothing in this list expires automatically.
  • Working copies of messages: removed 7 days after your assistant finishes with the message.
  • Sign-in sessions: refresh tokens expire after 30 days and are revoked when you sign out; their records are deleted with your account.
  • Push tokens: deleted when you sign out, when Apple reports that a token is no longer valid, or with your account. Each notification’s delivery record keeps its own copy of the token until that notification is deleted. [[CONFIRM WITH #153: that signing out unregisters the phone.]]
  • Abuse-protection counters: deleted after they expire.
  • Usage records: kept for cost and abuse tracking, with no set deletion date. They never contain message content, and when you delete your account they are unlinked from it.
  • Operational records: records of setting up and removing your assistant’s machine, and the log of administrative actions, are kept after account deletion, with no set deletion date. They contain your former account’s reference number but no email, name or content.
  • Server logs: kept by Fly.io for [[LOG RETENTION PERIOD]].
  • Backups: our database is backed up daily and each backup is kept for 5 days, so deleted data can remain in backups for up to about 5 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, Delete account. Deletion is available to everyone, wherever you live.

What happens right away

Your account is disabled and you are signed out on every device. Your sign-in sessions and your assistant’s access are revoked, your routines stop, queued messages and pending notifications are cleared, and outstanding invitations for your email are revoked.

What happens next

In the background, we delete your assistant’s machine. Once Fly.io confirms it is gone, we permanently delete your account record and everything linked to it: conversations, memories, routines, notifications (with the push-token copies kept for their delivery), uploaded documents, settings, sessions and push tokens. If a step fails, your account stays disabled and signed out. We retry automatically, and if that does not work, we finish the deletion manually. Email us if you would like confirmation that it is complete.

What remains

  • Backups, for up to about 5 days.
  • Usage records with no content, unlinked from your account.
  • Operational records that contain your former account’s reference number only.
  • Server logs, for the period described above.
  • On your device, the AI-notice record described in Data stored on your device.

Deleting individual items

You can delete individual memories in the app. To delete a specific conversation or uploaded document without deleting your account, email us. When we delete a conversation, we erase its messages, title and summaries; basic records such as timestamps remain until you delete your account.

Security

  • The app talks to our service only over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes. Refresh tokens and invitation codes are stored as hashes.
  • Our database is stored on a disk that Fly.io encrypts at rest.
  • Each account’s assistant runs on its own virtual machine.
  • Our main service’s logs are designed to exclude message content; error logs from your assistant’s machine may occasionally include fragments of it.

Your conversations, memories and document text are stored in our database in readable form so Peach can use them; they are not end-to-end encrypted. As the operator, we have administrative access to this database. We access it only when needed to run, secure or support the service, or when the law requires it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your personal data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Turn Peach’s abilities on or off, such as memory, routines, reading web pages and reading your documents.
  • Choose which kinds of actions Peach must ask you about first.
  • View and delete memories.
  • Sign out, or delete your account.

By email

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data in a machine-readable format;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete specific data, or your whole account;
  • restrict or object to how we use your data.

Email support@peach33.com from the address on your account. We may need to confirm the request is yours. We will respond within one month and will not charge you, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

If you are in the EU or UK

We rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide Peach under our Terms, including sending content to Microsoft Azure so Peach can answer you. The screen in the app that asks you to agree to this is a notice Apple requires; it is not a separate legal basis.
  • Consent: to use your waitlist email. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and keep usage, log and operational records.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

You need to give us your email address and password to create an account, and Peach cannot answer you without the AI processing described above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
